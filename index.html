SUBTOPIC 01 FUNDAMENTALS

What is Cybersecurity? (Detailed Overview)

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting internet-connected systems—including hardware, software, personal data, and mobile devices—from malicious digital attacks, unauthorized access, and damage. In simple terms, just as we lock our homes to keep burglars out, cybersecurity acts as a digital lock and shield for our computers, phones, and personal secrets on the web.

For school students, understanding cybersecurity is essential because we use the internet daily for online classes, gaming, video streaming, homework research, and chatting with friends. Modern cybersecurity relies on three fundamental pillars known as the CIA Triad:

  • ✓ Confidentiality: Ensuring your private information (passwords, photos, chat messages) is accessible only to you and authorized people.
  • ✓ Integrity: Protecting data from being illegally altered, modified, or tampered with by cybercriminals or viruses.
  • ✓ Availability: Ensuring that your computer systems, websites, and school portals work smoothly whenever you need them.

SUBTOPIC 02 PRIVACY & NETWORKING

Social Media Safety Guidelines

Social media platforms allow us to connect and share moments with friends, but oversharing or ignoring privacy settings can expose us to serious risks like identity theft and online stalkers.

  • 🔒 Strict Private Account Settings: Always keep your profile set to 'Private' so only approved friends can see your posts and photos.
  • 🚫 Never Accept Unknown Friend Requests: Online profiles can be fake; never accept requests from strangers.
  • 📍 Disable Live Location Tagging: Avoid posting your real-time school location, home address, or phone numbers.
  • 🔑 Keep Credentials Secret: Never share social media login passwords, even with close friends.

SUBTOPIC 03 SAFETY & WELLBEING

Understanding & Defeating Cyberbullying

Cyberbullying is the use of digital communication (social media, messaging apps, gaming lobbies) to intimidate, harass, humiliate, or target someone repeatedly. Examples include sending mean messages, posting fake rumors, creating mock accounts, or leaking private chats.

🛡️
The 4-Step Bully Defense Formula: 1. Don't Respond: Bullies seek reactions. Staying calm starves them of attention.
2. Take Screenshots: Save evidence of offensive messages with dates.
3. Block & Report: Use platform report buttons to block the bully immediately.
4. Tell a Trusted Adult: Inform parents, teachers, or school counselors right away.

SUBTOPIC 04 IDENTITY & SCAMS

Financial Security & Personal Info Protection

Cybercriminals frequently target students and parents to steal sensitive credentials such as credit card numbers, UPI PINs, Aadhaar numbers, and One-Time Passwords (OTPs).

  • 🔐 OTP Confidentiality: Never disclose an OTP (One Time Password) to anyone over phone calls, SMS, or WhatsApp. Bank officials never ask for OTPs!
  • ⚠️ Beware of Phishing Links: Do not click on suspicious links claiming you won cash prizes, free smartphones, or lottery rewards.
  • 💳 Safe Online Payments: Only use trusted payment gateways with your parents' permission.

SUBTOPIC 05 NETWORK SECURITY

Public Wi-Fi Networks: Dangers & VPN Safety

Free public Wi-Fi networks in airports, cafes, or parks are convenient, but they lack encryption. Hackers on the same network can execute "Man-in-the-Middle" (MitM) attacks to eavesdrop on your unencrypted network traffic.

  • 🚫 Avoid Sensitive Logins: Never access bank accounts, shopping apps, or school portals on open public Wi-Fi.
  • 🛡️ Use a VPN (Virtual Private Network): A VPN encrypts all data sent from your phone or laptop, rendering it unreadable to snoopers.
  • ⚙️ Turn Off Auto-Connect: Disable 'Auto-Connect to Open Wi-Fi' in your phone settings.

SUBTOPIC 06 STRANGER DANGER

Cyber Grooming Awareness (Stranger Danger)

Cyber Grooming occurs when an online predator pretends to be a friend, peer, or helper to build trust with a young person, gradually manipulating them into sending private photos or meeting offline.

  • 🚩 Red Flags: Strangers giving extravagant gifts, asking to keep secrets from parents, or requesting private photos.
  • 🛑 Zero Offline Meetings: Never meet an online stranger in real life without your parents accompanying you.
  • 🗣️ Speak Up Without Fear: If someone online makes you feel uncomfortable, tell your parents or teachers immediately. You are not in trouble!

SUBTOPIC 07 ESPORTS & GAMING

Online Gaming Safety & Account Protection

Online multiplayer games are fun, but in-game voice chat lobbies, unauthorized mods, and trade scams expose gamers to cyber risks.

  • 🎮 Use Gamertags / Pseudonyms: Never use your real name, birth year, or school name in your gaming handle.
  • 🚫 Avoid Unofficial Game Mods: Downloading illegal cheats, hacks, or free skin generators often installs spyware and trojans on your PC.
  • 💰 In-App Purchase Safeguards: Always obtain parental permission before making in-game microtransactions.

SUBTOPIC 08 DIGITAL ETIQUETTE

The T.H.I.N.K. Checklist Before You Post

Remember: "The Internet Never Forgets!" Once a photo or comment is posted online, anyone can screenshot and save it forever. Before clicking 'Publish' or 'Send', run your content through the 5-point T.H.I.N.K. filter:

  • T Is it True? Avoid spreading rumors, fake news, or unverified gossip.
  • H Is it Helpful? Will your post build someone up or tear them down?
  • I Is it Inspiring? Does it convey positivity and respect for others?
  • N Is it Necessary? Is there a genuine need to share this publicly?
  • K Is it Kind? Would you be comfortable showing this post to your parents or teachers?

SUBTOPIC 09 WEB BROWSING

Safe Website Browsing & HTTPS Security

When visiting websites for research or entertainment, always inspect the URL address bar to ensure your connection is encrypted.

  • 🔒 Look for HTTPS & Lock Icon: Safe websites begin with https:// (the 'S' stands for Secure). Avoid unencrypted http:// websites when submitting data.
  • 🔍 Inspect Domain Spelling: Watch out for typo-squatted fake domains like goog1e.com or paypa1-update.org.
  • 🚫 Block Malicious Pop-ups: Never click on flashing browser pop-ups warning "Your PC has 5 viruses! Click here to fix."

SUBTOPIC 10 ACTIONABLE RULES

Master Cyber Do's & Don'ts Checklist

✅ CYBER DO'S (ALWAYS DO THIS)
  • ✓ Enable Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA / 2FA) on all your accounts.
  • ✓ Use unique, complex passwords for every single platform.
  • ✓ Keep your Operating System, Antivirus, and Apps updated regularly.
  • ✓ Cover your laptop webcam when not in use.
  • ✓ Log out of public or school computers after finishing your session.
❌ CYBER DON'TS (NEVER DO THIS)
  • ✕ Never save passwords on shared or public web browsers.
  • ✕ Never plug unknown USB flash drives into your laptop.
  • ✕ Never share your OTP, credit card CVV, or passwords with anyone.
  • ✕ Never download pirated software, movies, or cracked games.
SUBTOPIC 11 LEGAL RIGHTS

Legal Rights & Emergency Cyber Helplines

Cyberbullying, financial fraud, identity theft, and stalking are serious criminal offenses punishable under law (such as the Information Technology Act, 2000 and POCSO Act in India, alongside global cyber laws).

  • 📞 National Cyber Crime Toll-Free Helpline: Call 1930 immediately to report financial cyber fraud within the first golden hour.
  • 🌐 Official Complaint Portal: Register anonymous complaints at cybercrime.gov.in.
  • ⚖️ Legal Rights for Minors: Minors are protected against digital stalking, harassment, and unauthorized photo sharing. Law enforcement agencies have specialized Cyber Crime Cells to investigate and apprehend offenders.
